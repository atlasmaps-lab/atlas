# Atlas Compare — FINAL (13 Nov 2026)

Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/QKa5JxJoxCnDScAZvFfUTJ. Source: `project/Main.dc.html` (template `Main.tpl.html`).

One Compare page (a sheet over the map, opened from the ATLAS menu, search or a place's card) with four views behind a switch at the top: **Table · Side by side · Head to head · Over time**.

- Top: "Compare", one italic line, the view switch, ×. Below: Places chips (each place keeps its own colour everywhere, UK grey for reference) + "Add a place"; Measures chips with ✓ + "Add a measure".
- **Table:** places down, measures across; value plus a bar in each cell; click a heading to sort (↓/↑); UK row shaded "for reference"; a note on sources and bases; actions: Make a chart ⌄, Copy as CSV, Excel, ☆ Workspace, Put it on the map, Layout ⌄ (places × measures / places × years / measures × years); "Patterns between two measures" as a closed row.
- **Side by side:** one small chart per measure in the same place order, UK as a dashed line, one plain line ("London is highest at …; the North East is lowest at …").
- **Head to head:** two places (A/B tags on the chips; click a place to swap in), mirrored bars, one sentence per measure chosen by the size of the gap ("2.4 times …", "£4.9bn more abroad than …", growth stated for both).
- **Over time:** lines for every place, £ or 2010 = 100, end labels that never overlap, events marked (2020), caption in plain words; "before inflation" stated.
- Motion: rows/panels rise in turn; bars fill in; lines fade in one after another. PT Serif only; gold for controls; light and dark.
